# CIQUAL liquids: a "100 ml = X g" unit from their composition

- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md); request in chat

## Context

The user: "CIQUAL milk has been entered as ml and not g. Add conversions for these cases". CIQUAL is per 100 g and
its foods had no units, so an amount in ml couldn't be converted. CIQUAL gives no densities.

## Decision

- Liquids get "100 ml = X ± Δ g" (`external.js ciqualDraft`). Liquids are the subgroups soups, milk, cream, water,
  non-alcoholic and alcoholic beverages, vegetable and fish oils, and baby milks/beverages, plus any food named
  "drink" or "beverage". Names with powder, dehydrated (not reconstituted), granules, freeze-dried, ground, or
  leaf/leaves are left out.
- Density from composition (ideal mixing, Choi & Okos component densities): 1/ρ = Σ wᵢ/ρᵢ over water, fat, protein,
  carbohydrates, fibre and alcohol. Anything left to 100 g counts as carbohydrates. If water isn't given, it's
  whatever's left (for oils, the fat is); below 50 g, the food is taken as dry and gets no ml unit.
- Error: 3% (milk comes out 1.019–1.053 vs a real ~1.03); 5% for spirits (water and alcohol shrink when mixed,
  e.g. vodka −4%) and when the water was guessed.
- Computed in the app, not in `ciqual.json`, so `scripts/ciqual.py` is unchanged.

## Consequences

- 459 CIQUAL foods get the unit. Components and consumptions that point to a CIQUAL food pick it up straight away;
  copies saved before this don't have it.

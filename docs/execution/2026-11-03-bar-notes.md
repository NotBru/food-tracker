# Dashboard bars: every number a note on the bar; the budget a segment

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Homescreen)

## Context

The user asked for slimmer dashboard rows (style "bars"), with every number as a note above or below the bar: the
value, the ends of its range, the target's min and max. Keep Δ Budget for calories, tried as a segment between the
near ends of the estimate and the target. Web only for now; the Android port comes later (`android/README.md` Next port).

## Decision

- Row: name and unit in a 72 px column; then a lane of notes, the bar (8 px, was 10), a lane of notes. About half the
  height of the old row (big value, "± Δ", range line, target line).
- Above the bar: the interval's low end, the value (bold, the nutrient's colour, `~` when estimated), the high end (in
  the range colour). With no error, only the value. Below: the min and/or max, muted; the bar's tints already say
  under / ok / over, so the ✓ ↓ ↑ mark is dropped here. The lane below is always there, so rows stay evenly spaced.
- Placement: each note centred on its place on the bar. Notes in one lane that would overlap become a block, centred
  where its notes want it on average and kept inside the bar (`spreadNotes`). Widths come from canvas `measureText`
  in the notes' own fonts, so it's one render pass.
- Budget (calories with a max): a thin line with end ticks from the interval's high end to the max, in a third lane;
  the label "Δ 1415" sits on it, green, or red with "Δ −300" when the high end is past the max (the segment then runs
  max → high end). Same amount as before (max − high end). When the segment is shorter than its label, the label
  hides it.
- Rows without a target: the grey bar used to be full (no scale, so no place for notes); it's now drawn on
  `barScale` with no target (the interval's top at 80%), grey and hatched like the others.
- What goes where on the bar is in `dashboard.js` `barNotes` (shared, for the port); the lanes and spreading are
  `Home.jsx`. Gauges are unchanged.

## Alternatives considered

- **Budget segment in the lane under the bar, with the min and max** — collides with the max note, which sits at one of its ends.
- **Leader lines from a pushed-aside note to its place** — clutter on an 8 px bar; pushes are small in practice.

## Consequences

- Ported to Android the same day: [android-bar-notes](2026-11-03-android-bar-notes.md).

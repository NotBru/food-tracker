# The app in Spanish (EN | ES), and CIQUAL's and USDA's foods with Spanish names

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Config screen: General; V2 reference databases)

## Context

Asked: Spanish names for the USDA and CIQUAL foods, a toggle in the web app between English and Spanish, and the
whole app translated.

## Decision

- **Texts keyed by their English** (`src/i18n.js`): `t('Save')`, with `{name}` placeholders (`t('made {when}', …)`;
  a React element as a value gives an array of parts), `plural(n, one, other)`, and `translated(list)` for the
  constant lists whose labels are read everywhere (NUTRIENTS, dashboard ROWS, body fields, pie macros, food
  categories, dashboard styles): getters, so a label is translated when read. The Spanish is one flat dictionary,
  `src/es.js`; a missing text falls back to the English, and in dev warns once in the console.
  `node scripts/i18n-check.mjs` lists the literal texts missing from it (`--unused`: the entries nothing uses).
  Keyed by the English rather than by ids, so the code reads as before and a new text works in English before
  its translation exists.
- **Argentine Spanish** (voseo: "Probá", "Escribí"; "palta", "frutilla", "manteca"), as the app's user is in
  Argentina. Macro initials: P · G · C.
- **The language is this device's own**, like the selected user: a `config` row `language` ('en' by default), not
  synced (sync.js LOCAL), so two devices on one backup can differ. The toggle was first in Config's General block (EN |
  ES); moved, as asked, to a button on the homescreen's top row, left of Config: it shows the current language
  ("EN", "ES") and a tap switches to the other (with more languages, the next). Compact (less padding) and not shrinking, like Config: in the default row it fits
  at 360 px; with the version pill on, the user's name gives way first, as before.
  Dates' weekday names follow it (es-AR).
- **Reference foods' Spanish names** (`scripts/es/`): `ciqual.tsv` and `usda.tsv` (`<code><TAB>name`) and
  `groups.json`, merged by `merge.py` into `public/ciqual.json` and `fdc.json` as one more, last, column and a
  `groupsEs` beside `groups` (ciqual.py and fdc.py call it after rebuilding a table; it's idempotent). All 3444
  CIQUAL and 8094 USDA foods have one. +0.16 MB and +0.55 MB, uncompressed.
  A food is made in the current language (its name, and its group under it); its other names (English, French,
  Spanish) are kept as `otherNames` and searched too, so "palta" finds avocados in English and "avocado" in Spanish.
  "crudo/a/os/as" count as plain like "raw" and "cru".
- **Translated by agents** (Sonnet, batches of a few hundred, with a guide: Argentine names, the US cut names kept in
  brackets where there's no equivalent, brand names and codes left), checked by hand in part (samples, the common
  foods, the beef cuts: consistently "Carne vacuna, …").
- **The `database` attribute** is the app's own key (CIQUAL / USDA / OFF): shown as "base" in Spanish; the user's
  attribute keys are shown as typed.
- **Backup's messages** come from backup.js and backup-crypto.js, which stay free of imports (Node runs the latter);
  they're translated where shown, `t(e.message)`.
- The log-food skill (`.claude/skills/log-food/scripts/food.mjs`, not in the repo) reads the lists through
  `translated([`, and `find` searches the Spanish names too.

## Alternatives considered

- **An i18n library (i18next, FormatJS)** — the app needs plain strings, a few plurals and two languages; a
  dictionary and three helpers do it with no dependency.
- **Ids as keys** — every text would turn into an id in the code, and English into one more dictionary.
- **The language synced with the config** — it's a person's and a screen's choice; the same backup may be read on a
  device that's someone else's.
- **Translating the names at run time (an API)** — slow, online only, and different each time.

## Consequences

- Not translated: units (g, cup, tbsp, "1 cup, cubes": a food's own units, typed or from USDA), what the user typed
  (foods, attributes, user names, notes), Open Food Facts' products, and the backup's server error texts.
- The Android app stays English: its Kotlin screens aren't ported (the shared logic, run through `src/native/core.js`,
  never sets a language). Porting them is follow-up work.
- A new text in the code needs its line in es.js (i18n-check finds it); a food added to CIQUAL or USDA needs its line
  in scripts/es/ (until then it shows its English name).
- Local variables named `t` shadow `t()`: renamed where they did (forms.js, Targets.jsx, Body.jsx).

Checked: i18n-check (398 texts, 0 missing); build; Playwright at 390 px over ~20 screens in Spanish (home, Config,
consumptions and their editor, foods, food info and the label, a reference food, targets, body, notes, users,
breakdown, explore, graphs, backup, Open Food Facts): no errors nor missing texts, nothing overflowing; English
unchanged; the toggle switches live both ways; the reference tables only gained the column and `groupsEs`.

## Open questions

- Translations of the less common foods weren't all read: a wrong one can be fixed in scripts/es/*.tsv and merged.

# Explore a nutrient across the databases; "provenance" renamed "database"

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md)

## Context

The user asked for an explore button on a nutrient's dashboard breakdown: the food-info energy chart, a list of the
foods in the databases, and checkboxes for the databases ("user" plus the values of the provenance attribute).
Also: rename "provenance" to "database" everywhere, the user's export included.

## Decision

- 🔍 at the top right of the breakdown opens `#/explore/<nutrient>` (`screens/Explore.jsx`).
- Foods: own foods plus CIQUAL's while the config lists it; concoctions left out (batches, not foods). Ranked (user's
  request) by the share of the food's energy the nutrient's low end reaches: low end × kcal/g ÷ energy from the
  macros' means (the chart's total). Nutrients without energy: low end per 100 kcal; energy itself: low end per
  amount. Foods without energy go last. Per 100 g, or per their own amount when they don't convert to grams.
- The screen is the window's height: chart (at most 45%, scrolls), search, foods (the rest, at least 80 px), switches
  (at most 24%), so the switches are always in view.
- Tapping a food puts it in the chart on top (the top-ranked one by default); its name opens its Info tab.
- A food's database is its `database` attribute (any case), `user` without one. All on at first.
- Bottom, split in two: database switches | food switches ("Logged only": foods any user has a consumption of; not
  counting foods only logged as a recipe's component). Switches are checkboxes with `role="switch"` (`input.switch`).
- A search box above the list scrolls it to the best-ranked match (folded, like the food search), centred; Enter
  picks it for the chart.
- The list and the checkboxes scroll on their own.
- `provenance` → `database` in external.js and the Android port (code and tests). The user's export got the same
  rename (55 attributes, `Provenance` included) in `food-tracker-updated.json`, not committed. Databases already in
  a browser are not migrated: re-import.
- The energy chart's legend numbers may now wrap: with ranges they overflowed a phone's width (Explore and Info).

## Alternatives considered

- **Rank per 100 g** — the first version; replaced at the user's request.
- **Registered energy as the denominator** — near-zero-energy foods (diet cola) reached shares of 400%+.

## Consequences

- The Info tab's nutrient table still overflows a 390 px screen slightly (its range column).
- Android tests were edited but not run.
- Per 100 kcal, near-zero-energy foods (mineral waters) top the list for minerals.

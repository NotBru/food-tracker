# Weight: weigh-ins, a weight time graph, and maintenance calories from them

- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **Status:** accepted; name and menu place superseded by [body-measurements](2026-10-24-body-measurements.md)
- **Instructions:** none (not in [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md)); request in chat

## Context

The user got scales and asked to "Add weight", after Claude offered a quick log, a time graph with its 7-day
average band, and an automatic estimate of their real maintenance calories (logged intake against the weight trend).

## Decision

- **Data.** A `weights` table (db v13): `{userId, datetime, kg}`, per user like consumptions; deleted with the user;
  in exports and imports (`TABLES`). Older exports import with no weigh-ins; no format change was needed.
- **Weight screen** (`/weight`, Home: a "Weight" button with + beside Targets): weigh-ins newest first, tapped to edit
  or delete in a sheet (weight in kg, comma accepted, and time; a new one is now, from the last weight). `?new`
  (the +) opens a new one straight away. On top: the 7-day average and the maintenance estimate.
- **A day's weight** is the mean of that logical day's weigh-ins. **7-day average:** the mean of the days among d−6..d
  that have a weigh-in, its interval ± the standard error of that mean (spread / √n) at the config's coverage. Water
  swings make single days noisy, so this is what's read.
- **Maintenance** (`weight.js maintenance`), over the last 28 days:
  `maintenance = mean logged kcal/day − trend (kg/day) × 7700`.
  - The trend is the least-squares line through the daily weights; its error is the slope's standard error.
  - The intake is the per-day mean over days with consumptions from the first weigh-in to the day before the last
    (a morning weigh-in comes before that day's food), with its usual error. Unlogged days are left out, so a
    day forgotten doesn't count as a day fasted. The screen says how many days were logged.
  - The two errors add. The estimate needs weigh-ins on 4+ days at least 7 days apart. Until then it says what's
    missing.
  - 7700 kcal/kg is the common rule of thumb for weight lost or gained (mostly fat, plus water and lean tissue). Over
    a few weeks it's adequate; the real figure varies with what's being lost.
- **Time graphs:** a Weight chart right after Calories, once the user has any weigh-in. It has a dot per day weighed
  and the 7-day average band, drawn as the other charts' average. Its scale fits the weights (at least 2 kg tall),
  not from 0. It picks and drags with the others (the pointer handling is shared now). Its readout has no
  breakdown link.
- **Home menu:** four buttons. On a phone, Targets and Weight share the last row (tall phones too), so the dashboard
  keeps its height. Wide screens fit all four in one row.

## Alternatives considered

- **A Δ per weigh-in (scale resolution).** Day-to-day water swings (0.5–1.5 kg) dwarf a scale's 0.1 kg, and the
  spread between days already measures them, so it isn't asked for.
- **Weight bars from 0, like the nutrients.** A 0–80 kg column would hide every change that matters.
- **An exponentially smoothed trend** (as some apps do). The 7-day mean matches the rest of the graphs, and the
  regression gives the estimate an honest error.

## Consequences

- The Android port has none of this.

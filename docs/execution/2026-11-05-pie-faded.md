# Energy pie: outside the calorie target, the macros' split faded

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Homescreen: energy pie)

## Context

The user asked for the rings below the calorie min and above the max to show the same filling as the ones within,
less saturated, wherever the macros' targets can be met there, "so that it's easier to plan a course".

## Decision

- `pie.js`: `ringShares` no longer stops at the calorie target; it returns the shares and `calories: 'under' |
  'over' | null`. Only rings where the macros can't fit are `out` (#000421). `ringSegments` marks a ring's arcs
  `dim` outside the calorie target.
- Faded colours (`Home.jsx` `fade`): 40% of the saturation left, then mixed 42% with the tile's background. A first
  try (35%, mixed 75%) was about as light as the colours within the target, so the min and max circles didn't show.
- The readout says both: "below the calorie min · Protein 40%–64%".

## Consequences

Without macro targets, the faded rings are a faded green: calories are the only thing out of range there.

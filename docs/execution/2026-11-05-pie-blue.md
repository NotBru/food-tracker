# Energy pie: disallowed rings deep blue, not striped red

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Status:** accepted; calorie zones superseded by [pie-faded](2026-11-05-pie-faded.md)
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Homescreen: energy pie)

## Context

The user asked for the pie's "illegal" regions (outside the calorie target, or where the macros' targets can't fit)
to be plain deep blue instead of striped red.

## Decision

One flat deep blue, no stripes (first rgb 30 50 140; then #000421, the user's pick). It also ends the clash that made the stripes necessary: red read as
protein (coral). The legend's status colours (↓/↑ in red) are unchanged: they're the app's target status, not regions.

## Consequences

The dashed circles of the calorie interval (range cyan) stay readable on it.

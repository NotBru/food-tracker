# Energy pie: dashed lines at the targets; the room fans out from the eaten ring to the min

- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Homescreen: energy pie)

## Context

The user corrected [pie-square](2026-11-06-pie-square.md)'s lines: the dashed ones, constant-ratio, go up to the
calorie min at the macros' targets' minimums and maximums (with only protein's min and max set: exactly two, in the
square). No solid path; instead the background between the eaten ring and the min is no longer constant-ratio but
linear, from the eaten protein's edge out to its min and max at the min, "as if to guide the ratio" being eaten.

## Decision

- Dashed (`pie.js` `targetEdges`): the edges of each restricted macro's min and max on the calorie min's ring (the
  max's without a min; none without a calorie target), from 0 kcal up to it. On the pie each is mirrored (two lines
  per edge); the square shows one. Drawn also when nothing was eaten.
- Background, eaten ring → calorie min (`guideToMin`): each restricted macro's min and max share run from its share
  eaten now to its min and max share at the min, each straight in grams (in kcal of the macro, scaled to the ring's
  calories): eating the rest at one mix. Drawn as any ring (core, brighter flank, faded); the rest green.
  Never below what's eaten (a macro past its max at the min stays where it is). Inside the eaten ring: the history,
  unchanged; below the min with nothing eaten: the min's split, as before (the same lines, from 0).
- "Linear" read as straight in grams, like the path it replaces: a day eaten at one mix from now on traces the same
  curve, so the history inside the ring reads in the same terms. In the chart the edges curve a little.
- The readout there reads the fan ("1378 kcal: below the calorie min · Protein 24%–35%").
- Removed: `pathToMin`, `eatenEdges` (the split eaten now as lines).
- Checked in headless Chromium: protein min/max only and protein + fat, round and square, 1000 px.

## Alternatives considered

- **Straight lines in the chart** (shares linear in kcal): what "linear" may have meant; easy to switch to.

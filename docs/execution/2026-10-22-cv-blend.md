# Sampling CVs scaled by √3 for blended samples

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** superseded by [foundation-foods-cvs](2026-10-22-foundation-foods-cvs.md)
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md); model from [`docs/notes/error-handling.md`](../notes/error-handling.md)

## Context

[cv-fit](2026-10-22-cv-fit.md) left open whether to scale the SR28 CVs for USDA's blends of several items per
sample. The user asked for √3.

## Decision

`sampling` in `app/src/errors.js` multiplies every fitted CV by `BLEND = √3`; the fitted table stays as fitted.

## Alternatives considered

- **Scale the table itself** — keeps the CSV and the code apart; one factor is easier to change.

## Consequences

- Energy CVs: meat 8% → 14%, unknown 8% → 15%. A 309 g grilled vacío (797 kcal) goes from about ±110 to −172/+199
  at 90%.
- The CVs still describe samples of the food an entry describes, not how well the entry matches what was eaten.

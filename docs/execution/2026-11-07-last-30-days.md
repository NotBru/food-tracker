# Dashboard: a "last 30 days" window

- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Homescreen dashboard, Breakdown screen); request in chat

## Context

User: "Add a 'Last 30 days' to the dashboard".

## Decision

- A new window, `last30`, shown as **Last 30 days**, right after Last 7 days: daily → last 7 days → last 30 days →
  weekly → monthly. `?win=last30` in the URL; arrows, swipe and dots as for the others.
- Rolling like [last 7 days](2026-10-20-last-7-days.md): today and the 29 days before it. Both are now one rule,
  `ROLLING` in `time.js` (window → length), used by `windowDays`, the title and the breakdown.
- Figures as in the other long windows: per-day averages over the days with consumptions.
- The breakdown opened from it starts on the **Days** tab, as from Last 7 days
  ([last7-days-view](2026-10-22-last7-days-view.md)): 30 rows, largest first. Not asked for; it is what a rolling
  window's breakdown already does, and Ranked is one tap away.

## Alternatives considered

- **After Weekly** (ordered by length: 1, 7, 7, 30, ~30 days). Kept the two rolling windows together instead.

## Consequences

- Android: the window comes from the web engine (`native/core.js`), so it appears at the next port; the dots'
  placeholder before the first load is now 5.

## Verification

Headless Chromium with a local copy of the user's data: the header reads "Last 30 days · 2026-10-09 → 2026-11-07",
"per day, over 23 days with consumptions"; Next from Last 7 days goes to Last 30 days, then Weekly; Calories opens
the breakdown on Days. No console errors.

# Daily dashboard: tomorrow

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Homescreen); request in chat
- **Extends:** [day arrows](2026-10-16-day-arrows.md) (which stopped at today)

## Context

User: "Have a 'tomorrow' available too, just to see the calories bounds I'm gonna face."

## Decision

- The daily window's › now goes one day past today, to **Tomorrow** (title `Tomorrow · Wed 2026-10-21`), and is
  disabled there. Nothing further ahead: the point is tomorrow's bounds, and only the next day's are meaningful.
- Tomorrow's calorie range is the usual [weekly correction](2026-10-18-correction-per-bound.md): its 6 earlier
  days include **today, as eaten so far** (worst case, like any earlier day). So it's a forecast that firms up as
  today is logged; until today is complete, tomorrow's bounds look looser than they will end up.
- `?day=` in the URL accepts tomorrow too; a day further ahead falls back to today. At midnight a viewed
  "tomorrow" simply becomes today.
- On tomorrow, the Consumptions button and its + open tomorrow's day (as they do for a past day), so a planned
  meal can be logged there.

## Alternatives considered

- **Tomorrow assuming today ends at its own target** (or within its corrected range) — a different number from
  what tomorrow will actually show; using what's logged keeps one rule everywhere.
- **Any future day** — further days would stack assumptions about unlogged days; not asked for.

## Consequences

- Web app only, like the weekly correction itself.

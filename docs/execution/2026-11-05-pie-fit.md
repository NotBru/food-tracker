# Energy pie: the tiles beside it fill its height

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Status:** accepted
- **Instructions:** [`docs/instructions/design.md`](../instructions/design.md) (Homescreen: energy pie)

## Context

After [pie-switch](2026-11-05-pie-switch.md) the pie's tile wasn't square (the readout line under it) and only one row
of tiles sat beside it. The user asked: the pie's tile exactly square, only the pie; its width a whole number of tile
heights; the tiles that fit beside it (two columns or more), all one height, never shrunk; one column: the pie on top.

## Decision

- `Home.jsx` `useFitPie` measures, before paint, the tallest tile's own height H, the grid's columns and gap, then
  picks the pie's span: k columns (side S = k columns and their gaps) and n = ⌊(S + gap) / (H + gap)⌋ rows. Every tile
  gets height (S + gap) / n − gap ≥ H, so n rows beside the pie are exactly its side. Set as CSS variables
  (`--pie-cols`, `--pie-rows`, `--tile-h`); measured again at each render and when the dashboard's width changes.
- Bars: k = 1 (the user's "as wide as any other nutrient"). Gauges: one gauge (≈150 px) is smaller than a gauge's
  height, so k grows until the tiles are at most 30% taller than their own height (1400 px wide: 3 gauges, 2 rows).
- Only one column (or gauges with no k that fits): the pie on top, the dashboard's width, square; tiles at H.
- The readout shows only while pointing, over the pie's foot (dark label); the "(eaten)" reading at rest is gone.
- Checked in headless Chromium (Playwright, the dev server) at 1400, 1000, 700 and 390 px, bars and gauges.

## Consequences

- Tiles grow up to 30% (bars: 73 → 87–88 px with the Δ budget line as the tallest) to fill the pie's side exactly.
- The tallest tile sets every tile's height (the calorie tile, with its Δ budget line).
